Hush Settings & Triage Policies

Privacy & Consent

Hush intercepts web notifications on allowlisted sites, redacts sensitive PII (emails, phone numbers, long digit sequences, URL queries) directly on your machine, and routes them through TypeSafe AI's Jev model or your local deterministic heuristics. Verification codes (OTP) are instantly routed to Now and are never stored.

Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)

Triage Thresholds

Higher values prevent interruptions unless confidence is extremely high.
Muting has high stakes: notifications are only muted when confidence exceeds this threshold.
